PubChem BioAssay curation and IC50-based drug repurposing

Muğla University. I extracted and curated bioassay data from PubChem BioAssay to train protein–drug IC50 activity models, and built a software workflow, generalizable to many proteins, that screens 110 million compounds for repositioning candidates (Useful Invention). I also developed a model for repurposed drug candidates that inhibit the non-globular CTNNB1 protein in the Wnt/β-catenin pathway in cancer (2nd ML4NGP Meeting, 2024).

OncoScreen: virtual-screening atlas for cancer genes

A Django web application that brings together the machine learning experiments, PDF reports and compound × bioassay heat maps of genes such as EGFR, BRAF and AR. Compounds are clustered by fingerprint similarity and bioassays by activity profile; both axes have dendrograms and cluster IDs. The pipeline uses stability selection, multi-classifier comparison, nested cross-validation and bootstrap confidence intervals.
